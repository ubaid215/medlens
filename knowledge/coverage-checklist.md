# Coverage Checklist — which images to make

Run **every condition** found in the screenshot through all 14 categories.
Include a category when the screenshot gives material for it **or** when it is standard,
well-established knowledge needed to understand the topic. Skip a category only when it
genuinely does not apply (e.g. no procedure exists for the condition).

Typical result: **8–14 images per condition.** Never stop at one image.

| # | Category | Include when | Image shows | Style |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Normal anatomy | Always, for the organ/system involved | 3D healthy organ, cutaway/cross-section, key structures labelled | 3D anatomical |
| 2 | Normal physiology | The disease disrupts a normal process | Normal function as arrows/flow (e.g. blood flow, conduction, filtration) | 3D + arrows |
| 3 | Causes & risk factors | Aetiology is known | Central organ with surrounding risk-factor icons | Infographic |
| 4 | Pathogenesis | Any disease with a mechanism | 3–5 numbered stages left→right, cellular/tissue level | Sequential panel |
| 5 | Pathology | Structural change exists | Split view: healthy vs diseased; gross ± microscopic inset | 3D split |
| 6 | Clinical features | Symptoms/signs exist | Human figure with callouts at affected sites | Body map |
| 7 | Investigations | Tests mentioned or standard | Typical lab/imaging/ECG findings, abnormal values highlighted | Clean panels |
| 8 | Diagnosis flow | A diagnostic pathway or criteria exist | Decision flowchart with criteria/scores | Flowchart |
| 9 | Non-drug management | Lifestyle, positioning, diet, physio, devices | Realistic scene or icon set | Realistic / infographic |
| 10 | Pharmacology | Any drug treatment | Drug class → target site in body → effect; "why chosen" | 3D + mechanism arrows |
| 11 | Procedures / surgery | A procedure or surgery applies | Realistic clinical/OR scene, steps numbered | Realistic scene |
| 12 | Complications | Complications are recognised | Organ/body with complication callouts, untreated course | 3D / body map |
| 13 | Follow-up & prevention | Monitoring or prevention exists | Timeline with monitoring points and targets | Timeline |
| 14 | Patient counselling | Always (one per condition) | Simple friendly explainer of condition + key advice | Simple illustration |

## Extra images (add when relevant)

- **Urgent Action** — goes first if any red flag is present (see `safety-rules.md`).
- **Differential diagnosis** — comparison grid of 3–4 look-alike conditions with key differences.
- **Classification / staging** — when a staging system is central (e.g. TNM, NYHA, CKD stages).
- **Treatment ladder** — when therapy escalates in steps (e.g. asthma, hypertension, pain).
- **Comparison image** — when the screenshot compares two conditions or two drugs.
- **Data from the screenshot** — when the screenshot has the user's own values (labs/ECG),
  one image visualises *those* findings against normal ranges.

## Splitting rules

- One teaching purpose per image. If a category is too dense (e.g. 6 drug classes),
  split it into 10a, 10b, 10c.
- Multiple conditions in one screenshot → one block per condition; shared anatomy is drawn once.
- Order: Urgent (if any) → 1 → 14, then extras where they fit logically
  (differential after #8, staging after #5, treatment ladder before #10).

## Plan size guide

| Screenshot content | Expected images |
|---|---|
| One condition, brief notes | 8–10 |
| One condition, detailed page | 12–16 |
| 2–3 conditions | 8–10 each (shared anatomy reused) |
| Single lab report / ECG | 6–10 (findings, causes, pathology, next steps, treatment) |
| Drug-focused slide | 6–10 (pharmacology split by mechanism, indications, side effects, monitoring) |
